BLOOM — Phase B: “Your Journey” Emotional Timeline

You are continuing development on Bloom, an offline-first emotional pregnancy companion app built with:

- React Native 0.76
- Expo 52
- Expo Router
- Zustand
- AsyncStorage
- Reanimated

Bloom is intentionally:

- emotionally calming
- gentle
- quiet
- low-pressure
- privacy-first
- offline-only

This is NOT:

- a productivity app
- a social app
- a clinical tracker
- a mental health analytics tool

Every design and UX decision should feel:

- warm
- breathable
- emotionally safe
- spacious
- human

Avoid:

- gamification
- metrics
- streak pressure
- emotional scoring
- charts/graphs
- dense dashboards
- productivity mechanics

IMPORTANT:
The app already has:

- onboarding
- pregnancy week system
- mood check-in
- unified ReflectionEntry architecture
- local persistence

Phase B now introduces:

“Your Journey”

This is the emotional memory layer of Bloom.

---

GOAL OF PHASE B

Create a soft, beautiful emotional timeline where users can revisit:

- reflections
- moods
- emotional moments
- supportive Bloom responses
- pregnancy memories

This should feel like:
"a gentle keepsake of the journey"

NOT:
"a history dashboard"

---

1. TAB STRUCTURE UPDATE

---

Current tabs:

- Home
- Week
- Mood
- Profile

Update to:

- Home
- Journey
- This Week
- Sanctuary

Rename:

- Mood → Journey
- Profile → Sanctuary

Journey becomes the emotional memory space.

Mood check-ins should still be accessible:

- from Home
- from Journey
- or as CTA cards

Mood no longer needs a permanent standalone tab.

---

2. CREATE JOURNEY SCREEN

---

Create:
"app/(tabs)/journey.tsx"

Purpose:
Display emotional reflections chronologically.

Structure should feel:

- calm
- spacious
- scrollable
- emotionally reflective

Avoid:

- dense feeds
- social-feed feeling
- analytics feeling

---

3. TIMELINE DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

---

Entries should appear as soft cards.

Each card may contain:

- mood
- date
- pregnancy week
- optional reflection text
- Bloom response

---

CARD EXAMPLES

Example 1:

Week 25 · Calm

"Something settled today."

Carry this calmness gently through your day.

---

Example 2:

Week 18 · Emotional

"I finally felt movement today."

Bloom:
What a beautiful moment to carry with you.

---

Example 3:

Week 31 · Anxious

"I've been feeling overwhelmed lately."

Bloom:
You are allowed to move slowly through difficult days.

---

4. GROUPING STYLE

---

Group entries softly by:

- month
  OR
- pregnancy week ranges

Examples:

"Second Trimester"

OR

"June"

DO NOT:

- over-segment
- create rigid date systems

---

5. EMPTY STATE

---

Very important emotionally.

If no reflections exist:

DO NOT use:
"No entries found"

Use:

"Your reflections will gather here gently over time."

Add a soft CTA:
"Check in today"

---

6. JOURNEY SCREEN UX

---

The screen should feel:

- emotionally warm
- meditative
- uncluttered

Spacing should breathe.

Cards should have:

- rounded corners
- subtle depth
- low-contrast backgrounds
- soft emotional color palette

Use existing Bloom design system.

---

7. OPTIONAL MEMORY HIGHLIGHTS

---

Add lightweight milestone support.

Examples:

- "First trimester complete"
- "Halfway there"
- "Viability week"
- "Due month approaching"

These should appear rarely and gently.

NOT:

- achievements
- badges
- rewards

Tone should feel reflective.

---

8. ADD "WRITE A FEW WORDS" ENTRY FLOW

---

From Journey screen:
Add subtle CTA:

"Capture a moment"

This should open:

- mood selection
- optional reflection
- Bloom response flow

Reuse existing ReflectionEntry architecture.

---

9. IMPORTANT UX CONSTRAINTS

---

DO NOT ADD:

- streaks
- mood charts
- graphs
- percentages
- emotional analytics
- trend scoring
- productivity framing
- pushy reminders

Bloom should NEVER feel like:
a self-optimization tool.

---

10. MOTION + INTERACTION

---

Animations should feel:

- slow
- soft
- breathing
- calm

Recommended:

- fade transitions
- subtle upward motion
- opacity sequencing

Avoid:

- sharp springs
- aggressive bounce
- flashy movement

---

11. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

---

Journey screen may eventually contain:

- months of entries

Implement:

- performant FlatList/FlashList usage

- lightweight rendering

But preserve emotional softness.

---

12. DATA + STORAGE

---

Use existing:
"ReflectionEntry"

Required retrieval methods:

- grouped timeline entries
- latest reflections
- reflections by week

Keep architecture modular.

- emotional tone
- visual softness
